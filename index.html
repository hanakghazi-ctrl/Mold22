How it works

From the first idea to the finished piece.

01

You tell us

Share what you are imagining: a sketch, a photo, a feeling or just the room it is for. One sentence is enough to begin.

02

We draw it

We come back with sketches, 3D drawings and material samples, together with a quote for the piece.

03

We make it

Once you approve the drawings, the piece is made in our workshop. You get progress photos along the way.

04

We bring it to you

We deliver and install, so the piece arrives where it belongs, ready to use.

Materials

What we work with.

Most pieces combine two or three. If yours needs something that is not on the list, ask us.

Wood

Plywood, MDF and veneers, cut, shaped and finished to order.

Solid timber

Oak, ash, beech and walnut, oiled, stained or lacquered.

Lacquer & paint

Any colour, matched to your brand or your room.

Metal

Steel, aluminium and brass, bent, welded or brushed.

Stone

Marble, travertine and terrazzo for tops and bases.

Acrylic & resin

Translucent screens, cast forms and coloured blocks.

Fabrics, leathers and bouclé for seating.
